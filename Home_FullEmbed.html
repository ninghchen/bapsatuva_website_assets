About BAPS

What we do

BAPS partners with organizations across the UVA community to solve real operational problems — often involving a technical component like a spreadsheet system, a piece of software, or a troubleshooting process.

Most members study systems engineering, though students of any major are welcome. Beyond service to the community, BAPS gives 1st-years early, hands-on exposure to systems engineering concepts with mentorship from upperclassmen on their project teams.
